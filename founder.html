FOUNDER & CEO

The person behind the sphere.

Anjali Srivastava

Founder & CEO, Aethera Sphere

Anjali Srivastava is the Founder and CEO of Aethera Sphere, a purpose-driven organization dedicated to advancing emotional intelligence, wellbeing and human potential. Inspired by the belief that true success is built on self-awareness, resilience and meaningful human connection, she founded Aethera Sphere to bridge the gap between traditional education and the essential life skills needed to thrive in today's world.

Through her vision and leadership, Aethera Sphere empowers individuals, educators and organizations to cultivate emotional wellbeing, lifelong learning and compassionate leadership — creating a lasting impact on both personal growth and collective progress.

"We founded Aethera Sphere to bridge the gap between traditional education and the essential life skills needed to thrive in today's world."
ANJALI SRIVASTAVA
